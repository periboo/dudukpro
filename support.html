DudukPro Support

Welcome to the support page for DudukPro.

DudukPro is an all-in-one practice companion built specifically for the Armenian duduk. It brings together tools for pitch, rhythm, audio study, exercises and care, all in one calm place.

Contact

If you need help, found a bug, or want to request a feature, email:

  • Email: dudukpro@markusdrayss.com

When reporting a problem, it really helps if you include:

  • Your device model (e.g. iPhone 13)
  • Your OS version (e.g. iOS 18.1 / Android 14)
  • What you were trying to do
  • What happened instead (and any error messages)

Common questions

I found a bug. What should I do?
Please email me with the details above. Screenshots or a short screen recording are very welcome.

I have an idea for a new feature.
I’d love to hear it. Tell me how you use the app and what would make your duduk practice easier.
